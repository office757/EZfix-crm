The Calendar canvas now overrides the more specific generic workspace canvas rule that made the background pale and hid the white SuperEZX artwork and headings. The original logo asset remains unchanged. The selected current date also overrides the legacy beige !important rule. Week styling follows the month palette, with compact mobile filters, blue current-day highlighting, blue/yellow/navy appointments and white schedule panels. The Day renderer is unchanged; week overlap geometry, drag/drop and role filters remain intact. Deleted jobs are excluded from the week.

Owner/office users can manage a technician portrait using Profile photo in the technician detail or editor. Uploads accept JPEG/PNG/WebP up to 10 MB, crop centrally to a 512px square JPEG, and store a private unique asset. A narrowly scoped RPC checks office authorization, target technician, object ownership, path, JPEG type and saved size, then updates only profile_photo_path and writes an audit record. Existing login, role, commission and app_data controls are preserved. Storage policies restrict portrait uploads to office staff and reads to office staff or the portrait's technician. The team list, month selected-day cards, week technician list and appointments show signed portraits, with initials when unavailable. Signed URLs are renewed without rerendering open editors. Removing a portrait clears its profile reference without deleting the stored asset.

Banking now includes Withdraw to bank. The owner-only flow opens the official Square balance page for Transfer Now, where Square shows the amount, destination and fees and the owner confirms the transfer. Existing provider-backed transfer history can then be refreshed. The current Square Payouts API exposes list/get/list-entry operations, not initiation; no direct withdrawal API, fake balance or transfer-success record is introduced. Direct in-app disbursement remains dependent on a supported provider integration.

Validation: full release gates; actual-module tests for private portrait signing/cache, invalid/foreign paths, role restrictions and truthful Square handoff; rolled-back database tests for owner save, office removal, denied technician mutation, own-only portrait reads and denied technician storage uploads. Browser visual verification is not claimed.
